ROUTE PLAYBOOK: Diablo II LoD 1.14, Normal, kill Baal.
Sources: MrLlamaSC's 18-part "Diablo 2 Speedrun Tutorials" (YouTube) and the speedrun.com/d2lod leaderboard.
You play a little SAFER than a speedrunner: same route and map logic, but keep potions and a town portal.

HOW MAPS WORK (the most useful knowledge)
- Maps are random, but the RULES are fixed. "Straight" = the direction the entrance area faces when you arrive.
  "Left"/"right" are relative to that, not to the screen. Three rights make a left: a path may wind, but the exit
  still faces the stated side.
- Outdoor maps are big squares/rectangles (ignore small cut-outs). There is never a second exit on the same edge
  as the one you came in through, and never an exit right next to the town exit.
- Dead ends: a block with a red Fallen group is a dead end. A light/glow in a dark area = a boss group (or a waypoint
  if it's a lamp with no rubble around it).
- Before leaving an area to come back later, open a town portal ("set a TP"), grab the next waypoint, then return.

EXPERIENCE AND MONEY
- Kill BOSS GROUPS and their minions (several times the experience). Skip single weak monsters; champion pairs
  and Tainted aren't worth the time. Lure big packs into a group, then turn and kill them all at once.
- Best experience: boss groups in the Forgotten Tower, beetles in Act 2 (Far Oasis, Canyon of the Magi),
  the Flayer Jungle in Act 3. Act 4 and 5 monsters give little; level earlier.
- Level targets: 2 before the Cold Plains, 4 in the Cold Plains, 5 leaving the Underground Passage, 6 in the
  Black Marsh, 12-13 leaving the Tower, 14 at Andariel, 18 entering Act 3, 20 leaving the Flayer Jungle.
  You MUST be level 20 for Act 5. If behind, farm beetles in the Canyon of the Magi.
- Gold: pick up rings, amulets, charms, chipped gems, throwing knives/javelins and heavier armor (chain mail and up)
  to sell. Rejuvenation potions sell for 200. Click every shrine (experience and skill shrines are huge),
  but avoid cursing archers while an experience shrine is active: a curse removes it.

SHOPPING AND BELT (shift/ctrl clicks)
- Sell fast: ctrl + left-click items in your inventory while trading.
- Buying potions: left-click repeatedly (faster than right-click). Shift + right-click a potion fills every belt
  column of that type. For a caster: 1 column of red (healing), 3 columns of blue (mana).
- Shift + belt key (e.g. shift+1) gives that potion to your mercenary.
- Early on, stamina is short: buy 2+ stamina potions, and toggle walk/run with r to recover stamina.
- Selling a Tome of Town Portal and buying it back refills it to 20 scrolls.

ACT 1 - Rogue Encampment
1. Blood Moor: pick up items to sell, kill boss groups, find the Den of Evil (cave) and the Cold Plains exit.
   Set a TP at the Den, get the Cold Plains waypoint, then come back. Den of Evil: kill every monster
   (quest log q shows how many remain), talk to Akara for a skill point.
2. Cold Plains: the Stony Field exit is in the MIDDLE of an edge; an exit near a corner is the Burial Grounds (skip).
3. Stony Field: the Cairn Stones and Rakanishu (worth killing) touch the path. Find the Underground Passage.
4. Underground Passage: the exit to the Dark Wood is straight ahead or to the right. Cave level 2 is to the left (skip).
5. Dark Wood: just follow the path to the Black Marsh. Black Marsh: find the waypoint, shrines and the Forgotten Tower.
6. Forgotten Tower: on EVERY level the exit is to the LEFT. An L-shaped room without windows always holds a
   boss group. Level 5: The Countess drops runes. Keep Tal and Eth for Stealth. Repeat the Tower
   (save and exit, waypoint to the Black Marsh) until about level 13 and you have Tal + Eth.
7. Monastery Gate -> Outer Cloister (waypoint) -> Barracks (check left first, then straight) -> Jail level 1 (waypoint;
   exit straight) -> Jail 2 (straight) -> Jail 3 (left) -> Inner Cloister -> Cathedral -> Catacombs.
   Catacombs level 2: the exit faces right of the waypoint. Level 4: Andariel (poison). Keep distance, drink potions.
   Then talk to Warriv to travel east.

GEAR GOALS
- Stealth = Tal then Eth (order matters) in a GRAY 2-socket body armor. Blue "socketed" items can't take runewords.
- Ral runes give fire resistance in a helm or shield, Tir gives mana per kill, topaz gems in a shield give
  lightning resistance (Act 2 beetles hurt). Strength up to 25 only (enough for a 12-slot belt).
- Leaf = Tir then Ral in a gray 2-socket staff (for fire skills). Faster cast rate on wands/rings is great for casters.

ACT 2 - Lut Gholein  (visit Cain to identify, Fara to repair, Lysander for potions)
- Every outdoor exit is in a CORNER of the map, and only in a "full" corner. If a corner has an indent or a long
  narrow notch, there is no exit there. On arriving, work out which corner you're in, then check the other full corners.
- Halls of the Dead (from the Dry Hills, with waypoint) level 3 chest: Horadric Cube. In the Halls, go LEFT
  (straight = the door opposite the wall at your back).
- Far Oasis (waypoint) -> Maggot Lair: level 1 exit right, level 2 right, level 3 straight across. Chest: Staff of Kings.
  Only completely round rooms are dead ends there.
- Lost City (waypoint) -> Valley of Snakes -> Claw Viper Temple: always go LEFT. Rooms with bookshelves are dead ends.
  Level 2: Amulet of the Viper on the altar. Open a TP first (dangerous). Then talk to Drognan, then Jerhyn.
- Cube Staff of Kings + Amulet = Horadric Staff. The order of these Act 2 pieces doesn't matter: do whichever is closest.
- Palace -> Harem -> Palace Cellar level 3 -> Arcane Sanctuary. Four arms; the Summoner is at the end of one.
  Teleport pads: take the one that goes FURTHER away. You don't have to kill the Summoner: click Horazon's Journal.
  Next to it are 6 tomb symbols. The correct tomb is the symbol NOT shown there.
- Canyon of the Magi (waypoint): kill beetles here if under level 18. In the tomb go LEFT; a room with 4 pillars in a
  cross has exits in all 4 directions. Put the Horadric Staff in the Orifice, open a TP, drink THAWING potions
  (cold resistance, they stack), then fight Duriel. Talk to Tyrael, Jerhyn, then Meshif (boat).

ACT 3 - Kurast Docks  (you're often broke here: pick up armor to sell)
- Spider Forest: stay on the LEFT side of the river. A double bridge means an entrance on the right side.
  Spider Cavern (chest = Khalim's Eye) is in a bottom corner. "Great Marsh skip": sometimes the Spider Forest
  connects straight to the Flayer Jungle. Look for a turn-off where the river bends oddly.
- Flayer Jungle: follow the river; entrances are on the OUTSIDE of river bends. Kill everything here (great
  experience). Flayer Dungeon: always LEFT. Level 3 chest = Khalim's Brain.
- Lower Kurast -> Kurast Bazaar (the Upper Kurast exit is diagonally opposite the entrance) -> Sewers level 1 -> level 2
  (it branches off a long side passage). Chest = Khalim's Heart. The Travincal exit is in the centre.
- Travincal: the first council member you kill drops Khalim's Flail. Cube Eye + Brain + Heart + Flail = Khalim's Will,
  then hit the Compelling Orb.
- Durance of Hate level 1: exit LEFT. Level 2: waypoint left, exit STRAIGHT. Level 3: Mephisto. Keep the council
  member near him away (he heals Mephisto). Dodge his lightning, keep a TP. Ranged trick: stand across the moat.

ACT 4 - Pandemonium Fortress  (hire or revive a mercenary; a Pike is a cheap big upgrade for an Act 2 merc)
- Outer Steppes exit: bottom-left corner, otherwise bottom-right. Plains of Despair: only 4 possible exits
  (top-right middle, top-left top, bottom, left corner). City of the Damned (waypoint) -> River of Flame: head up and
  right to the waypoint. If the quest log pops up, you're heading to the Hellforge: turn back.
- Chaos Sanctuary: open the 5 seals; three of them summon a boss group (Grand Vizier, Lord De Seis, Infector).
  Kill all three, then Diablo appears in the centre. His red lightning kills fast: stay out of its line.

ACT 5 - Harrogath  (potions are half price in Act 4; or kill Shenk for the first Act 5 quest to make them cheaper here)
- Bloody Foothills / Frigid Highlands / Arreat Plateau: go up; the passages show the way (top-left or top-right).
  Crystalline Passage: left. Glacial Trail: exit top-right. Frozen Tundra: straight across. Ancients' Way: top-left.
- Arreat Summit: the 3 Ancients (random gear, sometimes very strong). You can pull one away and fight one-on-one.
  Your mercenary helps a lot. Retreat to a TP if low.
- Worldstone Keep level 2 (waypoint; exit toward the bottom-right) -> level 3 (Death Lord packs are deadly: lure them away)
  -> Throne of Destruction. Baal sends 5 waves; kill each wave or pull it away from the throne. Wave 3: the boss
  (Bartuc) must follow you or the others won't. Then take the portal and kill Baal (stay out of reach of his tentacles).

SORCERESS BUILD (MrLlamaSC; the fastest class)
- Start with Charged Bolt (+ Fire Bolt), some Energy. Frost Nova at 6, Telekinesis around the Tower.
- At level 13, respec with Akara: 25 Strength, 50 Energy, rest Vitality; skills: Static Field 7, Telekinesis 1,
  Frozen Armor 1, Frost Nova 1, the rest Nova. Save a point for Teleport at 18.
- Fight: Static Field on big monsters/bosses (it takes a percentage of their life), Nova for packs, Frost Nova to slow.
